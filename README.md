# sr-releases

The installers for Recanic - Screen Recorder (called SR until version 0.2.50), by Ross O'Leary. Get the newest from the
Releases page: https://github.com/MooseDog76/sr-releases/releases/latest

## Privacy note

- **Who made Recanic:** Ross O'Leary. To ask about privacy, or to complain, email feedback@recanic.com. I reply within 30
  days. Never attach recordings or work files.
- **What's kept, and where:** your recordings, their notes and transcripts, and Recanic's settings - on your computer, and in
  your own server folder if you pick one.
- **What goes online:** Recanic asks GitHub (github.com) whether there's a new version of Recanic. Open Otter only opens
  Otter's website in your browser - Recanic never uploads anything.
- **Nothing is sent to me:** no account, no tracking, no adverts.
- **Not happy with my answer?** You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
