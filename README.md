# sr-releases

The installers for SR - Screen Recorder, by Ross O'Leary. Get the newest from the Releases page:
https://github.com/MooseDog76/sr-releases/releases/latest

## Privacy note

- **Who made SR:** Ross O'Leary. To ask about privacy, or to complain, write here on the Issues tab. I reply within 30 days.
  Never attach recordings or work files.
- **What's kept, and where:** your recordings, their notes and transcripts, and SR's settings - on your computer, and in your
  own server folder if you pick one.
- **What goes online:** SR asks GitHub (github.com) whether there's a new version of SR. Open Otter only opens Otter's
  website in your browser - SR never uploads anything.
- **Nothing is sent to me:** no account, no tracking, no adverts.
- **Not happy with my answer?** You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
